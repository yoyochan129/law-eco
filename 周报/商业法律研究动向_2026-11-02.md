Executive Summary Vanguard Investor Choice empowers individual investors, their financial advisors, and retirement and 529 plan sponsors to direct how their share of eligible funds are voted at company shareholder meetings. Investor Choice is an extension of our longstanding mission to take a stand for all investors.
