**Stock Lending and Pass-Through Voting**

- 作者：Doron Levit
- 摘要：Concerns about conflicts of interest have led to growing calls for asset managers to let fund investors cast votes directly at shareholder meetings. We study this reform, known as pass-through voting, in a model where market participants with heterogeneous preferences lend shares, trade, and vote. We show that it can move voting outcomes further away from investors' preferences, rather than closer, for a reason that has received little attention: the fund's manager retains control over share lending and may lend more once the votes are no longer his to cast.
