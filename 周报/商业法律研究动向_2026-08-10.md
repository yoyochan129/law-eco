*美联储紧急贷款*

- 作者：Richard Clarida, Kathryn Judge
- 日期：2026
- 摘要：The Federal Reserve established an array of innovative emergency lending facilities during the Great Financial Crisis and expanded the scope of its emergency lending yet further in response to the Covid-19 pandemic. This Article provides a retrospective of how the Federal Reserve used its emergency lending authority across these two episodes, identifying patterns and revealing some differences. It sheds light on the conditions that enabled the Federal Reserve to establish the facilities that it did, including the roles played by Congress and Treasury in providing the equity funding that made certain facilities possible. It shows how in each episode, the Federal Reserve supported a whole-of-government response meant to limit the damage inflicted by a massive shock to the economy while still maintaining its independence with respect to monetary policy.
